Indicator Measurement Standards & Data Governance

Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP)

Measures the aggregate monetary value of all final goods and services produced within a state's borders in a given financial year. Compiled by State Directorates of Economics and Statistics (DES) and reconciled by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) under current price series.

Effective Literacy Rate

Calculated exclusively for the population aged 7 years and older who can both read and write with understanding in any recognized Indian language. Raw crude literacy figures including infants are excluded pursuant to Census of India standard guidelines.

Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS)

Annual and quarterly nationwide workforce survey administered by the National Sample Survey Office (NSSO). Unemployment rate reflects Usual Status (Principal Activity Status + Subsidiary Economic Status) for working-age citizens aged 15 years and above.

Infant Mortality Rate (IMR)

The probability of a child dying before completing their first year of life, expressed per 1,000 live births. Sourced from the Sample Registration System (SRS) Bulletins published by the Office of the Registrar General of India.

Sex Ratio at Enumeration

Expressed as the number of females per 1,000 males residing within the jurisdiction. Serves as a vital demographic barometer for gender equity, survival ratios, and regional demographic transitions across decennial census milestones.

Geographical Area & Density

Territorial surface measurements supplied by the Survey of India and Ministry of Home Affairs. Used to calculate population density (persons per square kilometer) and assess land utilization efficiency across states.

Frequently Asked Questions about Data Explorer

Can I download custom filtered datasets from IndiaMetrix?

Yes. Clicking the 'Export CSV' button downloads the exact table view currently rendered on screen, formatted with clean headers and source attributions.

How often are the state figures updated?

Indicators are reconciled whenever official agencies (MoSPI, Census Commissioner, Reserve Bank of India) publish updated survey rounds or annual economic estimates.

Research Export & Programmatic Data Access

The IndiaMetrix Explorer supports high-volume tabular filtering and client-side CSV downloads for researchers, economists, and university classrooms. All datasets are validated against primary sovereign releases and conform to standard RFC 4180 CSV specifications with UTF-8 encoding.
