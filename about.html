About IndiaMetrix

India, Explained Through Data.

Notice of Independence: IndiaMetrix is an independent, non-governmental public data initiative. It is not affiliated, associated, authorized, endorsed by, or in any way officially connected with the Government of India or any state government or statutory authority.

The Genesis: Democratizing India's Public Numbers

India generates a breathtaking volume of demographic, industrial, macroeconomic, and infrastructure data across dozens of ministries, statutory statistical directorates, and international agencies. Yet, for decades, this immense wealth of knowledge remained locked behind complex ministerial PDF bulletins, fragmented portal databases, and inaccessible statistical tables.

IndiaMetrix was founded by data scientists, software engineers, and public policy researchers to bridge this civic gap. Our mission is to transform cold sovereign spreadsheets into interactive, accessible, pre-rendered, and rigorously verified intelligence for everyone—from high school students and civil services aspirants to university researchers, journalists, and curious citizens.

Our Foundational Editorial Standards

Zero Data Fabrication

We never synthesize, simulate, or extrapolate data points. Every metric displayed maps strictly to a sovereign publication.

Temporal Provenance

We strictly separate the original survey collection year from our database verification timestamp, ensuring total temporal clarity.

Universal Bilingualism

Information must not be gatekept by language. All pages, scorecards, and tools provide seamless dual-language support in English and Hindi.

Non-Partisan Objectivity

We present raw sovereign data without editorial distortion, political bias, or ideological spin. The numbers speak for themselves.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often are platform statistics updated?

Our automated ingestion pipelines monitor statutory publication releases weekly. Whenever MoSPI, the RBI, or NITI Aayog releases new survey rounds or revised estimates, dependent dashboards are regenerated synchronously.

Can educators and universities use IndiaMetrix in coursework?

Yes. We actively encourage educational institutions to integrate our interactive maps, calculators, and comparative datasets into economics, geography, public policy, and data science curricula.

How can I contribute or report an issue?

Visit our Contact & Data Verification Desk to submit errata with primary citations, or open an issue on our public GitHub repository.
