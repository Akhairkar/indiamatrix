About IndiaMetrix

India, Explained Through Data.

Notice of Independence: IndiaMetrix is an independent, non-governmental public data platform. It is not affiliated, associated, authorized, endorsed by, or in any way officially connected with the Government of India or any state government.

Our Mission

India produces vast volumes of socio-economic, demographic, industrial, and infrastructure data across hundreds of government departments, research surveys, and multilateral organizations. However, accessing and synthesizing this data is often difficult due to fragmented PDF reports, disparate portals, and inconsistent reporting formats.

IndiaMetrix was founded with a singular mission: to make verified public data about India accessible, intuitive, interactive, and actionable for everyone — students, researchers, policy analysts, journalists, business leaders, and curious citizens.

Our Core Principles

  • Zero Data Fabrication: We never invent or extrapolate statistics without clear documentation. Every data point maps directly to an official publisher.
  • Mandatory Provenance: Every metric clearly displays its reference year, publishing body, verification date, and official primary source link.
  • Temporal Honesty: We strictly distinguish between the underlying survey data year and our verification date. Older census data is never masked as 'latest'.
  • Bilingual Access: We are committed to providing equal data access in English and Hindi, ensuring accessibility across linguistic backgrounds.
  • Non-Partisan & Open: IndiaMetrix presents numbers objectively without political commentary or bias.

Primary Data Sources

Our platform aggregates structured data published by official government ministries and recognized international organizations, including:

  • Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner, India (Census)
  • Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI) (PLFS, National Accounts)
  • Reserve Bank of India (RBI) (Handbook of Statistics on Indian Economy)
  • NITI Aayog (SDG India Index, State Dashboard)
  • Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (MoHFW) (NFHS Reports)
  • World Bank & United Nations Statistics (Cross-country benchmarks)

How to Cite IndiaMetrix

Researchers, journalists, and students may cite IndiaMetrix data in reports and articles. Please reference both the primary official publisher and IndiaMetrix:

"IndiaMetrix. [Indicator Name] — [State/National Profile] ([Year]). Original source: [Publisher Name]. Retrieved on [Date] from https://www.indiametrix.in/"
