Global Comparative Insights

The Demographic Dividend

While China and the West face demographic aging and working-age contraction, India's median age is ~28.4 years. Over 65% of its population is in the working-age bracket (15-64), providing a multi-decade manufacturing and consumption advantage.

Digital Public Infrastructure

India processes over 46% of all real-time digital payments globally through UPI. Combined with Aadhaar identity and open APIs (India Stack), the country has achieved unmatched financial inclusion velocity at low transaction costs.

The Productivity Frontier

India's per-capita GDP of ~$2,500 contrasts with China's $12,500 and the US's $80,000. Closing this productivity gap through capital formation, logistics corridors, and skills upgrading defines India's path toward a developed economy.

Frequently Asked Questions: India vs World

What is India's GDP ranking among world economies?

India ranks as the 5th largest economy in the world by nominal GDP ($3.96 trillion in 2025 according to the World Bank), following the United States, China, Germany, and Japan. In Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) terms, India ranks 3rd globally.

How does India's population compare to China and the United States?

According to the UN and World Bank midyear estimates, India became the world's most populous country with approximately 1.43 billion people, surpassing China (1.41 billion) and more than quadruple the United States (335 million).
