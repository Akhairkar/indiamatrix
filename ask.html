Macroeconomy

What is India's GDP in 2025-2026?

According to the World Bank World Development Indicators, India's nominal GDP stands at $3.96 Trillion (Current USD). MoSPI estimates place domestic nominal economic output at approximately ₹272.41 Lakh Crore (2022-23 base series).

Demography

What is India's total population?

The last comprehensive decennial Census of India (2011) enumerated 1,210,854,977 (1.21 Billion) citizens. Midyear 2025 demographic estimates published by the UN Population Division and World Bank place India's resident population at 1.46 Billion.

Education

What is India's national literacy rate?

India's official effective literacy rate is 74.04% as recorded in the 2011 Census (82.14% for males and 65.46% for females). The National Statistical Office (NSO) 75th round survey estimated effective literacy at 77.7%.

Employment

What is the current unemployment rate in India?

According to the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) Annual Report (2023), the All-India unemployment rate among persons aged 15 and older stands at 3.1% (usual status).

State Leadership

Which state is the largest economic contributor?

Maharashtra leads all Indian states with a Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP) of ₹35.27 Lakh Crore (MoSPI 2022-23), contributing nearly 13% of national GDP, followed by Tamil Nadu (₹23.64 Lakh Cr) and Gujarat (₹22.67 Lakh Cr).

Social Indicators

Which state has the highest literacy rate and sex ratio?

Kerala ranks #1 nationwide in both metrics, achieving a 94.00% literacy rate and an enumerated sex ratio of 1,084 females per 1,000 males according to the Census of India 2011 records.
