Data Integrity & Verification Protocol

IndiaMetrix is committed to the highest standards of statistical accuracy. Because public policy and academic research depend on reliable numbers, we enforce a strict 48-to-72 hour verification service level agreement (SLA) on all reported discrepancies. Every data point on this platform is traced directly to primary sovereign repositories including MoSPI, RBI, Census of India, NFHS-5, and NITI Aayog.

1. Primary Gazette Evidence

Corrections must cite official government releases, state statistical handbooks, or statutory ministry dashboards. Secondary news reports are not accepted as sovereign benchmarks.

2. 48-Hour Rapid Audit

Once logged, our automated ingestion scripts re-pull the original tables to verify if recent provisional estimates were superseded by revised first or second estimates.

3. Public Errata Disclosure

All verified modifications are recorded in our public version logs, updating state profiles, indicator hubs, and historical benchmark databases simultaneously.

Academic & Media Citation Standards

Researchers, policy analysts, and journalists are encouraged to utilize IndiaMetrix data visualizations and statistical aggregates under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license. When citing IndiaMetrix in academic papers or media publications, please follow the standard citation conventions below:

APA Citation Format:

IndiaMetrix Research Group. (2026). National Socio-Economic & Demographic Profile of India: Consolidated Multi-Source Indicators. Retrieved from https://www.indiametrix.in

BibTeX Citation:

  title = {India Socio-Economic Indicators & State Analytics},

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly are reported data discrepancies resolved?

Reports accompanied by verified primary gazette or statistical report links are validated and corrected within 48 to 72 hours. An automated audit pipeline rebuilds all affected indicator rankings and comparison tables immediately.

What official sources are acceptable for submitting updates?

We accept primary documentation published by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), Reserve Bank of India (RBI), Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (NFHS), NITI Aayog, and statutory state economic survey directorates.

Is there an API or raw CSV export available for research?

Yes. Every state profile, indicator dashboard, and historical series provides raw JSON and CSV download buttons. Furthermore, researchers can access all curated datasets directly through our open GitHub repository under open data licenses.

Rapid Ingestion & Sovereign Errata Guarantee:

Every inquiry submitted through this desk is logged in our audit tracker. When errors are verified against primary gazettes or MoSPI publications, corrections are pushed to our production build within 48 to 72 hours, automatically updating all dependent state scorecards, indicator tables, and comparisons across the entire platform.
