Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Telangana?

Telangana's population was recorded at 3.5 Crore according to the Census of India 2011.

What is the literacy rate of Telangana?

The literacy rate of Telangana is 66.54% as per the 2011 Census enumeration.

What is the Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP) of Telangana?

Telangana's GSDP is estimated at ₹13,02,371 Cr (Current Prices, 2022-23) published by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI).
