State Profile & Intelligence

Nagaland

Explore verified demographic, economic, healthcare, and infrastructure indicators sourced from official government publications.

Nagaland is a major constituent state/UT of the Republic of India. According to official Census of India records and Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) publications, Nagaland has an enumerated population of 19.7 Lakh (0.16% of India) and an estimated Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP) of ₹35,680 Cr (2022-23). The state maintains an effective literacy rate of 79.55% (+5.51% vs national average) and an unemployment rate of 5.4% (PLFS 2023). Its sex ratio stands at 931 females per 1,000 males.

Strategic Socio-Economic Analysis

Economic Scale & Contribution

Nagaland reports a GSDP of ₹35,680 Cr (MoSPI 2022-23), contributing approximately 0.13% to India's aggregate output. This economic productivity shapes state fiscal capacity, infrastructure capital expenditure, and per-capita income dynamics.

Human Capital & Literacy

At 79.55%, Nagaland's effective literacy rate is +5.51% relative to the national benchmark of 74.04%. High literacy correlated with female educational attainment serves as a primary foundation for workforce productivity and social mobility.

Demographic Weight & Landmass

With 19.7 Lakh residents across 16,579 sq km, Nagaland accommodates 0.16% of India's population on 0.50% of its total geographical landmass, driving regional resource allocation, urbanization patterns, and public governance.

Public Health & Healthcare Delivery

An Infant Mortality Rate (IMR) of 23 per 1,000 live births (-5 vs national average of 28) and a sex ratio of 931 (-12 vs 943) highlight the state's maternal-child health infrastructure and gender balance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Nagaland?

Nagaland's population was recorded at 19.7 Lakh according to the Census of India 2011, accounting for 0.16% of the nation's total enumerated population.

What is the literacy rate of Nagaland?

The literacy rate of Nagaland is 79.55% as per the 2011 Census enumeration, which is +5.51% compared to the national average of 74.04%.

What is the Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP) of Nagaland?

Nagaland's GSDP is estimated at ₹35,680 Cr (Current Prices, 2022-23) published by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), representing 0.13% of national GDP.

What is the unemployment rate in Nagaland?

According to the Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) 2023 by MoSPI, the unemployment rate in Nagaland is 5.4% (usual status, age 15+), compared to the All-India rate of 3.1%.

What is the sex ratio and infant mortality rate in Nagaland?

Nagaland recorded a sex ratio of 931 females per 1,000 males (Census 2011) and an Infant Mortality Rate (IMR) of 23 per 1,000 live births (SRS Statistical Report 2020).
