Frequently Asked Questions about State Comparisons

How does the IndiaMetrix State Comparison Engine work?

The engine allows users to select any two Indian states or union territories and perform a synchronized, side-by-side benchmark across 7 official indicators: Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP), population, literacy rate, unemployment, infant mortality rate, sex ratio, and geographical area.

Which Indian state has the largest economy (GSDP)?

Maharashtra holds India's largest state economy with an estimated GSDP of ₹35.27 Lakh Crore (MoSPI 2022-23 Current Prices), followed by Tamil Nadu (₹23.64 Lakh Cr), Gujarat (₹22.67 Lakh Cr), and Karnataka (₹22.41 Lakh Cr).

Where does the state comparative data come from?

Every metric links directly to verified government sources: Census of India (Registrar General of India) for population and literacy, MoSPI for GSDP and PLFS employment surveys, and the Sample Registration System (SRS) for infant mortality.

Can I compare districts or perform economic equivalence projections?

Yes. You can explore granular district profiles in the Districts Directory, compare state economies to sovereign nations in the Economic Equivalence Tool, or project educational targets in the Literacy Calculator.
