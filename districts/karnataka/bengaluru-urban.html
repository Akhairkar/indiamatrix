Bengaluru Urban

Bengaluru Urban is an administrative district located in the state of Karnataka, India. According to the decennial Census of India 2011 enumeration, Bengaluru Urban recorded an official population of 9,621,551 (9.62 million), which accounts for approximately 15.7% of Karnataka's total population. The district maintains an effective literacy rate of 87.67%, which is 13.63% higher than the national benchmark of 74.04%. With an enumerated sex ratio of 916 females per 1,000 males, Bengaluru Urban provides vital demographic insights into urban and regional development patterns within Karnataka.

Demographic Analysis & Core Takeaways

Demographic Concentration

Bengaluru Urban houses 15.75% of Karnataka's total population (9.62 million out of 6.1 Crore). This highlights its role as a key regional population and economic center within the state.

Educational Attainment

With an effective literacy rate of 87.67%, Bengaluru Urban stands +13.63% relative to the All-India benchmark (74.04%) and +12.31% compared to Karnataka's state average (75.36%).

Sex Ratio & Gender Balance
