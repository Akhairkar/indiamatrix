District Demographic Intelligence

Nagpur

Nagpur is an administrative district located in the state of Maharashtra, India. According to the decennial Census of India 2011 enumeration, Nagpur recorded an official population of 4,653,570 (4.65 million), which accounts for approximately 4.1% of Maharashtra's total population. The district maintains an effective literacy rate of 88.39%, which is 14.35% higher than the national benchmark of 74.04%. With an enumerated sex ratio of 951 females per 1,000 males, Nagpur provides vital demographic insights into urban and regional development patterns within Maharashtra.

Demographic Concentration

Nagpur houses 4.14% of Maharashtra's total population (4.65 million out of 11.24 Crore). This highlights its role as a key regional population and economic center within the state.

Educational Attainment

With an effective literacy rate of 88.39%, Nagpur stands +14.35% relative to the All-India benchmark (74.04%) and +6.05% compared to Maharashtra's state average (82.34%).
