District Demographic Intelligence

Lucknow

Lucknow is an administrative district located in the state of Uttar Pradesh, India. According to the decennial Census of India 2011 enumeration, Lucknow recorded an official population of 4,589,838 (4.59 million), which accounts for approximately 2.3% of Uttar Pradesh's total population. The district maintains an effective literacy rate of 77.29%, which is 3.25% higher than the national benchmark of 74.04%. With an enumerated sex ratio of 917 females per 1,000 males, Lucknow provides vital demographic insights into urban and regional development patterns within Uttar Pradesh.

Demographic Concentration

Lucknow houses 2.30% of Uttar Pradesh's total population (4.59 million out of 19.98 Crore). This highlights its role as a key regional population and economic center within the state.

Educational Attainment

With an effective literacy rate of 77.29%, Lucknow stands +3.25% relative to the All-India benchmark (74.04%) and +9.61% compared to Uttar Pradesh's state average (67.68%).
