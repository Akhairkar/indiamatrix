District Demographic Intelligence

Ernakulam

Ernakulam is an administrative district located in the state of Kerala, India. According to the decennial Census of India 2011 enumeration, Ernakulam recorded an official population of 3,282,388 (3.28 million), which accounts for approximately 9.8% of Kerala's total population. The district maintains an effective literacy rate of 95.89%, which is 21.85% higher than the national benchmark of 74.04%. With an enumerated sex ratio of 1027 females per 1,000 males, Ernakulam provides vital demographic insights into urban and regional development patterns within Kerala.
