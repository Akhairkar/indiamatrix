Understanding India's District Administration Architecture

In India's federal structure, the district (headed by a District Magistrate, Collector, or Deputy Commissioner) serves as the pivotal nexus for executing public development schemes, census enumerations, health monitoring, and law enforcement. With over 750 districts across 28 states and 8 union territories, district-level data provides granular intelligence essential for regional infrastructure planning, corporate supply-chain siting, and socio-economic research.

All demographic indicators presented on IndiaMetrix are verified against official gazette publications from the Office of the Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India (Ministry of Home Affairs).

Frequently Asked Questions about Indian Districts

How many districts are there in India?

As of 2026, there are over 780 administrative districts in India, having increased from 640 districts enumerated in the 2011 Census due to subsequent administrative reorganizations across states like Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, and Punjab.

Which is the most populous district in India?

According to the 2011 Census, Thane in Maharashtra was the most populous district with over 1.1 Crore citizens (later bifurcated into Thane and Palghar). Today, districts like Bengaluru Urban (9.62M) and Pune (9.43M) rank among the largest metropolitan population clusters.
