Population Trend

India stands as the world's most populous sovereign democracy and the fifth largest global economy. As of 2025–2026, the country combines a population of 1.46 billion with an annual gross domestic product exceeding $3.96 trillion. Supported by rapid digital payments scaling through the Unified Payments Interface (UPI) and expanding formal workforce participation (3.1% PLFS unemployment rate), India's socio-economic indicators reflect a historic transformation across public health, financial inclusion, and infrastructure capital expenditure.

Frequently Asked Questions about India's Headline Numbers

Why is the official literacy rate reference year 2011?

The full decennial Census of India was last completed in 2011. While sample surveys like the NSO 75th round provide interim estimates (~77.7%), the Census figure of 74.04% remains the official statutory baseline under government governance norms until the next census round is published.

How does IndiaMetrix ensure zero data fabrication?

Every single metric links directly to its primary source gazette, states its exact data year, and discloses the precise methodology. The platform does not use interpolation or synthetic projections for published headline statistics.
