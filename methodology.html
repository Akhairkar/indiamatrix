Statistical Methodology & Data Governance

An exhaustive technical reference on how IndiaMetrix ingests, reconciles, audits, and presents sovereign socio-economic data with mathematical integrity.

1. Sovereign Authority

We rely exclusively on official state statistical gazettes, constitutional ministries, and multilateral institutions. We reject unverified third-party estimates or algorithmic projections.

2. Zero-Fabrication Rule

If official data for a territory or year is unavailable, we explicitly state its absence. We never fabricate, interpolate, or synthesize artificial figures to fill visual gaps.

3. Temporal Transparency

Every metric is timestamped with its exact survey cycle or base year (e.g. 2011-12 series, NFHS-5 2019-21, PLFS 2022-23) so analysts know the precise historical baseline.

The 7-Phase Automated Validation Pipeline

Every byte of data published on IndiaMetrix passes through a seven-stage automated integration pipeline (executed via Python and GitHub Actions) before reaching public servers:

Phase 1: Ingestion & Checksum Verification

Raw datasets from MoSPI, RBI, and Census APIs are pulled into version-controlled staging environments with cryptographic SHA-256 hashes to guarantee data immutability.

Phase 2: Administrative Unit Standardization

India's state and UT boundaries have evolved (such as the reorganization of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh in 2019, and Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu in 2020). We map all historic data to standardized LGD (Local Government Directory) codes.

Phase 3: Schema & Type Strictness

Values are categorized into strictly bounded data types: percentages are bounded to [0.0, 100.0], population counts must be positive integers, and monetary metrics must state current vs constant currency units.

Phase 4: Statistical Outlier & Anomaly Detection

Automated delta detectors compare new series against prior decadal bounds. Any sudden year-on-year shift exceeding 3-sigma standard deviations triggers manual inspection before merging.

Phase 5: Bilingual Normalization

All indicator definitions, column headings, and methodology notes are synchronized across dual language attributes (`data-en` and `data-hi`) using standard Devanagari terminology approved by the Commission for Scientific and Technical Terminology.

Phase 6: Static Site Generation (SSG)

To eliminate client-side loading latency and prevent SEO thin-page degradation, Python build scripts pre-render full HTML tables, KPI scorecards, and JSON-LD structured schemas at build time.

Phase 7: End-to-End Link & Integrity Audit

A headless crawl validates 100% link reachability across all 76 published pages, ensuring zero broken 404 links, verified canonical URLs, and schema validation.

Macroeconomic Deflators & Base-Year Splices

Measuring national and state economic output requires distinguishing between nominal price inflation and real physical output expansion. When examining GDP, GSDP, and per capita income across IndiaMetrix, please note the following methodological frameworks:

Current Prices (Nominal)

Valued at the market prices prevailing during that specific financial year. Useful for fiscal calculations, tax-to-GSDP ratios, and inter-state share of total sovereign output.

Constant Prices (Real, 2011-12 Base)

Adjusted for inflation using the Gross Domestic Product deflator relative to the benchmark fiscal year 2011-12. This metric reflects true volume growth in goods and services.

Purchasing Power Parity (PPP $)

Utilized on our World Comparison Hub. PPP converts currencies based on the purchasing power of one international dollar in India relative to the United States, compensating for domestic price differences.

Methodology Frequently Asked Questions

Why do some state numbers differ between Central and State budgets?

State Economic Surveys often report 'Advance Estimates' or 'Quick Estimates' before final reconciled figures are published by MoSPI in subsequent years. IndiaMetrix always adopts the most recent statutory reconciliation and explicitly labels whether an estimate is provisional (PE) or revised (RE).

How can researchers access the machine-readable dataset archive?

Every indicator hub, state profile, and ranking table provides one-click CSV and JSON exports. Academic departments and open-data researchers can clone our entire verified repository directly via GitHub under the CC BY 4.0 license.

How do I submit an erratum or methodological query?

Submit reports via our dedicated Contact & Data Verification Desk. All submissions accompanied by primary gazette evidence are processed under our 48-to-72 hour verification SLA.
