Methodology & Trust

How we source, verify, and present data.

1. Primary Sources

IndiaMetrix relies exclusively on official and recognized institutional sources. We do not use unverified third-party estimates.

  • World Bank Open Data
  • Census of India
  • Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI)
  • Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
  • NITI Aayog

2. Data Verification Pipeline

All data entering our system undergoes an automated and strict data validation pipeline:

  • Schema Validation: Ensuring consistent formatting and structure.
  • Type Checking: Differentiating between percentages, absolute numbers, and indices.
  • No Fabrication: If data for a specific year is missing from the primary source, we do not interpolate or guess the value. We show the most recent verified figure and explicitly state its year.

3. Currency & Inflation

Where GDP or economic output is compared, we strive to clarify whether figures are in Current USD, Constant USD, or INR. Users can switch between these metrics in the Explorer tool when the source provides both.

4. Corrections

Despite automation, official datasets themselves sometimes issue revisions. When a primary source updates past data (e.g., revised GDP estimates), our pipeline pulls the latest revised figures automatically during our scheduled builds.
