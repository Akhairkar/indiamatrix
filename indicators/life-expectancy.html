27-Year Longevity Expansion

Average life expectancy at birth in India has risen from 45.2 years in 1960 to 72.0 years in 2023, driven by sanitation, vaccination, and maternal care.

👶

Infant Mortality Plunge

Infant mortality has fallen from over 160 per 1,000 in the 1960s to under 28 nationally, with pioneering states like Kerala matching European standards.

🏥

Institutional Delivery Growth

Institutional births now exceed 89% nationally under national health mission initiatives, substantially reducing maternal mortality.

🩺

Epidemiological Transition

Public health focus is expanding from infectious diseases toward management of hypertension, diabetes, and cardiovascular wellness.

Measurement Standards & Public Policy Significance

Public socio-economic indicators provide the empirical foundation for fiscal devolutions, demographic planning, and infrastructure capital allocation across India's federal union. Sourced from constitutional reporting bodies and international development institutions, this indicator is tracked to measure inter-state convergence, human capital accumulation, and quality-of-life advancements across all 36 States and Union Territories.

Data Integrity & Revision Cycle

Annual indicator estimates are updated synchronously following official releases by primary statistical authorities. Unverified third-party estimates or algorithmic extrapolations are strictly rejected.
