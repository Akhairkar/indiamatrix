Official Data Sources Directory

Every statistic displayed on IndiaMetrix is traced to a primary publisher. We prioritize official statistical agencies of the Government of India and accredited multilateral organizations.

Methodological Transparency: IndiaMetrix never masks historical survey years as current figures. While our automated pipeline checks for official updates daily, the baseline reference years reflect the actual survey or reporting period.

Census of India

Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner, India
Decennial Survey

Primary source for official district and state population, literacy rates, sex ratios, and household demographics. Most recent full nationwide census was conducted in 2011 (the 2021 exercise was delayed).

Categories: Population, Demographics, Literacy Official Portal →

Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI)

Government of India
Annual / Periodic

India's central statistical authority responsible for National Accounts Statistics (GDP, GSDP), Periodic Labour Force Surveys (PLFS unemployment and workforce participation), and Index of Industrial Production (IIP).

Categories: Economy, GSDP, Employment, Poverty Official Portal →

Reserve Bank of India (RBI)

Central Bank of India
Monthly / Annual

Publisher of the definitive Handbook of Statistics on the Indian Economy, Handbook of Statistics on Indian States, banking credit metrics, currency flows, inflation, and public debt statistics.

Categories: Banking, Finance, State Finances Official Portal →

NITI Aayog

National Institution for Transforming India
Annual Index

Policy think tank of the Government of India producing the SDG India Index, Aspirational Districts Programme rankings, state health index scores, and innovation indices.

Categories: Governance, Social Development, SDG Benchmarks Official Portal →

Open Government Data Platform (data.gov.in)

National Informatics Centre (NIC), MeitY
Continuous

National portal facilitating open data access across union ministries and departments under the National Data Sharing and Accessibility Policy (NDSAP).

Categories: Cross-Ministry Datasets, Infrastructure, Transport Official Portal →

Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (MoHFW)

Government of India
Periodic (NFHS-5)

Publisher of the National Family Health Survey (NFHS), Sample Registration System (SRS) bulletins for Infant Mortality Rate (IMR), Maternal Mortality Ratio (MMR), and life expectancy estimates.

Categories: Healthcare, Nutrition, Maternal & Child Health Official Portal →

World Bank Open Data

World Bank Group
Annual

Internationally standardized indicator repository used for cross-country comparisons (India vs China, USA, peers) and multi-decadal historical trends (1960–present).

Categories: India vs World, Historical GDP, Life Expectancy Official Portal →

National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB)

Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India
Annual

Publisher of Crime in India annual reports, accidental deaths and suicides statistics, and prison statistics.

Categories: Public Safety, Law Enforcement Official Portal →

Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change (MoEFCC)

Government of India / Forest Survey of India
Biennial

Publisher of the India State of Forest Report (ISFR) providing verified state-wise forest cover, tree canopy assessments, and wildlife conservation metrics.

Categories: Environment, Forest Cover, Biodiversity Official Portal →
