Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At IndiaMetrix, accessible from https://www.indiametrix.in/, our highest priority is respecting and safeguarding the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information recorded by IndiaMetrix and how we use it.

1. No Personal Data Collection

IndiaMetrix does not require user registration, logins, or accounts. We do not ask for or store your name, phone number, physical address, or financial information. You can explore all datasets, state profiles, rankings, and charts anonymously.

2. Local Browser Storage (localStorage)

We use your browser's local storage solely to remember client-side preferences across sessions:

  • Language preference (im-lang: English or Hindi).
  • Theme preference (im-theme: Dark or Light).

This information never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

3. Standard Log Files & Analytics

Like most websites, our static hosting providers automatically record standard web server logs (such as IP address, browser type, referring pages, and timestamp). These logs are used purely for diagnosing operational errors, monitoring system performance, and preventing malicious DDoS traffic.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Cookies (Google AdSense)

IndiaMetrix partners with Google AdSense to display contextual advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on prior visits to our website and other websites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. External Links to Government Sources

IndiaMetrix contains links to official primary publishers (such as data.gov.in, censusindia.gov.in, mospi.gov.in). Once you click an external link, you are subject to the privacy practices of that respective government or institutional portal.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about our Privacy Policy or data handling practices, please contact us via our official contact page.
