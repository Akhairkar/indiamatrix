Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Effective Date: August 2026 • DPDPA & Global Compliance

IndiaMetrix operates under a strict privacy-by-design architecture. We believe that exploring public statistical indicators should be a fully private, anonymous, and un-tracked civic activity. This Privacy Policy details the exact boundaries of how information is processed when you visit our website.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection (No Logins, No Profiles)

IndiaMetrix does not require user accounts, email sign-ups, or social logins. You can access every state profile, comparison tool, demographic calculator, and historical chart anonymously. We do not collect or store names, mobile numbers, Aadhaar numbers, biometric identifiers, physical addresses, or financial payment details.

2. Client-Side Preferences & LocalStorage

To deliver a seamless user experience across browsing sessions, our frontend JavaScript saves two lightweight configuration keys directly inside your browser's local sandbox storage (window.localStorage):

  • im-theme: Remembers your preferred interface theme (Dark or Light mode).
  • im-lang: Remembers your selected reading language (English or Hindi).

These values never leave your device, are never transmitted across the network to our servers, and contain zero personally identifying tokens. You may clear your browser's cache or local storage at any time to delete these values.

3. Advertising & Google AdSense Disclosures

To support server infrastructure costs and keep sovereign statistical datasets free for all citizens, IndiaMetrix partners with Google AdSense to serve non-intrusive contextual advertisements. Google, as a third-party advertising vendor, utilizes cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites across the web.

Users can manage or opt out of personalized ad targeting at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or through the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out portal at www.aboutads.info.

4. Web Server Logs & Infrastructure Security

Like all websites connected to the global internet, our edge content delivery network (CDN) and static web servers maintain automated access logs containing IP addresses, browser user-agents, referring URLs, and request timestamps. These logs are used exclusively for mitigating distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, analyzing bandwidth capacity, and ensuring 99.9% platform uptime. Log data is purged periodically and is never correlated with individual users.

5. India Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023) Compliance

IndiaMetrix operates in full compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) enacted by the Parliament of India. Because we do not process, monetize, transfer, or store personal digital data, visitors are protected from invasive digital surveillance while using our civic platform.

Do Not Track (DNT) Signals & Cookie Consent:

IndiaMetrix respects modern browser privacy features including Do Not Track (DNT) and Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals.
