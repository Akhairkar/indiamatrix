2. Temporal Lags & Official Revisions

Official statistics are subject to structural survey schedules and periodic revisions by publishing agencies:

  • Decennial Census Data: The 2011 Census represents the most recent universal decadal headcount completed in India. Subsequent demographic figures are official statistical survey projections (such as NFHS-5 or MoSPI technical group estimates) and are explicitly marked as such.
  • Macroeconomic Revisions: GSDP and NSDP figures are continually revised by state directorates from Advance Estimates (AE) to Provisional Estimates (PE), First Revised (FRE), and Final Estimates. IndiaMetrix displays the latest published revision cycle.
  • Verification Timestamps: The 'Verified' dates shown across profile cards indicate the date our automated pipeline reconciled the record against official releases, not the date the survey was physically conducted.
