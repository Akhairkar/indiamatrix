2. Data Timeliness & Survey Lags

Government statistical exercises operate on distinct publication frequencies. For example:

  • Census figures: Conducted decennially; the 2011 Census remains the most recent nationwide full headcount.
  • PLFS / NSSO surveys: Published periodically by MoSPI.
  • GSDP estimates: Subject to revision from 'Quick Estimates' to 'Revised' and 'Final' figures over subsequent fiscal years.

IndiaMetrix explicitly labels the reference year for every metric. The date of platform verification indicates when our pipeline checked the record, not that a new survey was conducted on that day.
